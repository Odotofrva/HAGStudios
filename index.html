Our Story

The inspiration behind Hacim Alyn Indie Gaming Studios comes from a lifelong love of retro gaming, unforgettable titles, and the dream of creating worlds that leave an impact on players for years to come.

Retro Roots

Built From Early Gaming Memories

Hacim Alyn Indie Gaming Studios was born from a deep love for gaming that started at an early age. Growing up on retro experiences and legendary titles like Zelda and Pokémon, gaming became more than entertainment — it became inspiration, imagination, and possibility.

Early memories of playing major titles, following iconic game releases, and even taking part in gaming tournaments through places like Blockbuster helped shape a real passion for the culture of games. Those moments created a lasting respect for adventure, competition, creativity, and worldbuilding.

Why We Build

A Father’s Reason To Create

Today, that same passion is being transformed into original 2D and 3D games through Hacim Alyn Indie Gaming Studios. The mission is not only to build fun, stylish, and futuristic game experiences, but also to leave behind something meaningful.

At the heart of it all is one powerful reason: the desire for my kids to one day be able to say, “Our father made a game that went popular.” That dream fuels the work, the creativity, and the commitment to building games that can inspire others the same way great games inspired me.
